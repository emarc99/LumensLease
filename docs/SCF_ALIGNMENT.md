# 🚀 Alignment with the Stellar Community Fund (SCF)

> **Initiative:** [Stellar Community Fund (SCF) Build Awards](https://communityfund.stellar.org/)  
> **Target:** Commercial dApp / Real-World Financial Inclusion Track

---

## Strategic Fit & Value Proposition

LumensLease is architected as an end-to-end commercial product designed to drive organic consumer and financial activity on the Stellar network. It directly targets one of the most acute, high-volume financial pain points across emerging African markets: **predatory upfront rental demands and unrecovered caution/security deposits.**

---

## Core SCF Alignment Pillars

### 1. High-Impact Real-World Utility (Not Speculation)
* Millions of renters in Lagos, Abuja, Nairobi, and Accra lose their caution/security deposits annually to landlord bad faith, or are forced to borrow at predatory interest rates to pay 1–2 years of upfront cash.
* LumensLease replaces this broken system with an autonomous, non-custodial Soroban escrow that holds deposits safely and streams rent without middlemen.

### 2. Catalyst for Soroban Smart Contract Adoption
* Demonstrates a consumer-facing, real-world utility application built natively on Soroban.
* Proves that Soroban contracts can power daily living agreements (tenancy contracts, escrow locks, multi-party dispute arbitration) beyond standard DeFi swaps.

### 3. Sustainable Stablecoin Velocity (Stellar USDC & cNGN)
* Rental contracts create persistent, recurring volume on the Stellar ledger.
* Renters and landlords transact in **Stellar USDC** and local currency stablecoins (such as **cNGN**), driving steady on-chain volume, trustline creation, and network transactions.

### 4. Financial Inclusion & Verifiable On-Chain Credit History
* Over 80% of African renters are excluded from formal banking credit because rental payments are treated as informal cash transactions.
* By anchoring lease agreements with SHA-256 hashes and recording successful, timely rent disbursements on the Stellar ledger, LumensLease builds a verifiable on-chain credit and address history for every tenant.

### 5. Synergy with Modern Stellar Primitives
* **Account Abstraction:** Leverages the Soroban Passkey Kit so users interact via mobile biometrics (FaceID/TouchID) without crypto complexity.
* **Price Oracle Reliability:** Uses Reflector Oracle for transparent, real-time NGN/USDC currency conversions.
* **Anchor Rail Interoperability:** Uses SEP-24 / SEP-6 to connect local bank transfers and mobile money directly into the escrow.
