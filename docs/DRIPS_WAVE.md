## How to Contribute

1. **Pick an Issue:** Browse open issues tagged with `Stellar Wave` on GitHub.
2. **Claim on Drips:** Connect your GitHub account at [drips.network/wave](https://www.drips.network/wave) and register for the Stellar Wave.
3. **Submit a Pull Request:** Link your PR to the issue with a clear description, test coverage, and documentation updates.
4. **Maintainer Review:** Once reviewed and merged into `main`, your contribution points will be credited on the Drips Wave leaderboard.
